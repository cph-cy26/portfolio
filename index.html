HØRT

A digital concept developed within a client-defined problem space, designed to help young people with hearing loss take ownership of their hearing health through a clear, accessible and behaviorally informed digital experience.

Role

Project lead, research, UX & concept development

Type

Student Project, 2025 Concept Development

Approach

Design Sprint (Google Ventures)

Problem

Young people with hearing loss often struggle with understanding and taking ownership of their hearing health, and may feel uncomfortable or uncertain about how to talk about their condition.

Through research, we identified a lack of accessible and relatable tools that support both awareness and open dialogue around hearing health in this target group.

Insights

The transition from medical care to independent life leaves young people with hearing loss without the support they need.

Relatable experiences, trustworthy information and safe spaces are key to reducing stigma and enabling ownership of their hearing and well-being.

Concept & Approach

Building on our research, the concept evolved into an “Onboarding to Adulthood” experience. After their final meeting, users are introduced to the app HØRT, designed to support young people with hearing loss in taking ownership of their hearing and social life.

Key features include:

Calendar reminders synced with hearing aid routines
Event notifications to connect with peers aged 18–25
Safe spaces to learn, share experiences and reduce stigma

The concept supports the transition from medical care to independent everyday life in a structured and relatable way.

Solution

The solution was designed as a mobile app experience supporting young people with hearing loss in their transition to independent everyday life.

The app combines practical tools with social features, allowing users to manage their hearing routines, connect with peers, and access relatable information in one place.

Value

The concept creates value by supporting users in taking ownership of their hearing in a more independent and confident way.

By combining practical tools with relatable experiences, the solution reduces stigma and makes hearing health a more natural part of everyday life.

Ownership

Users gain structure for daily hearing routines and feel more in control.

Connection

Peer-focused events and shared experiences reduce isolation.

Confidence

Relatable information and safe spaces help normalize hearing health.

HORISONT

Our concept is based on insights showing that skilled workers aged 30-39 perceive pension as abstract and overwhelming, despite having clear visions for their future life.

Due to limited mental capacity and the long-term nature of pension planning, engagement is low and often only occurs during job changes.

Based on this, we developed Horisont to help the target group understand the consequences of acting on their pension today and take responsibility for their future.

WHIRL

Whirl is a Danish proof-of-concept app designed to support young adults with anxiety through planning tools, a supportive chatbot, and evidence-based coping methods.

Developed through extensive research and expert collaboration, Whirl prioritizes empathy, ethics, accessibility, and user safety, offering a free, culturally relevant supplement to professional treatment.
