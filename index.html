HØRT

HØRT is a digital concept developed within a client-defined problem space, designed to help young people with hearing loss take ownership of their hearing health through a clear, accessible and informed digital experience.

Role

Project Lead
UX Design
Concept Development

Type

Client Center for Hørelse & Balance
Student Project

Approach

Design Sprint
Agile Process

Taking ownership is hard

Transitioning to adulthood at 18, young people with hearing loss are suddenly left to navigate the healthcare system on their own, requiring them to take full initiative for their own hearing care.

This shift occurs during a vulnerable life stage where taking responsibility for a chronic condition can feel overwhelming. Furthermore, many struggle with the social stigma or insecurity of communicating their hearing needs to peers.

What we found

The transition to adulthood often leaves young people with hearing loss without a vital safety net. To foster ownership of their well-being and reduce social stigma, there is a critical need for relatable experiences, trustworthy information, and safe spaces.

Our research reveals a significant gap: a lack of accessible tools designed to facilitate awareness and open dialogue within this specific demographic.

Onboarding to adulthood

Building on our research, the concept evolved into an "Onboarding to Adulthood" experience. After their final meeting, users are introduced to HØRT, designed to support young people with hearing loss in taking ownership of their hearing and social life.

The concept supports the transition from medical care to independent everyday life in a structured and relatable way.

Routines

Calendar reminders synced with hearing aid routines — so nothing gets forgotten.

Community

Event notifications to connect with peers aged 18–25 who get it.

Safe spaces

Places to learn, share experiences and reduce the stigma around hearing health.

The app

The solution was designed as a mobile app experience supporting young people with hearing loss in their transition to independent everyday life.

The app combines practical tools with social features, allowing users to manage their hearing routines, connect with peers, and access relatable information in one place.

Ownership

Users gain structure for daily hearing routines and feel more in control.

Connection

Peer-focused events and shared experiences reduce isolation.

Confidence

Relatable information and safe spaces help normalise hearing health.

HORISONT

Horisont is a web-based tool designed to transform pension planning from a distant obligation into an engaging priority for the younger generation. By simplifying complex data, Horisont empowers users to visualize their future self.

Role

UI Design
UX research

Type

Client Forte Advice // F&P
Student Project

Approach

Interviews
Figma Make

Lack of interest in the future

The Danish pension industry manages 4.5 trillion DKK, yet consumer engagement and trust remain critically low. Most people lack insight into their savings and the long-term consequences of inaction, leading to future financial insecurity.

This project explores how to bridge the gap between providers and policyholders, empowering Danes to take ownership of their pension and understand the urgency of acting today.

What we found

Target Group: Danes aged 30–39, with a focus on skilled workers. While this group shared vivid dreams of retirement - ranging from rural tranquility to global travel - there is a profound disconnect between their aspirations and their actions. Our research showed that while they value financial freedom and security, pension planning remains an abstract and overwhelming task.

The primary "pain point" is the gap between knowledge and action: in a life stage dominated by immediate priorities, long-term financial planning is sidelined. The result is a persistent sense of uncertainty about whether their current path will actually lead to the retirement they imagine.

A look into the future

Our proposed concept focused on transforming pension planning into a visual and intuitive experience to increase user engagement. By utilizing gamification, we stripped away the formal complexity of the industry, making the impact of today's decisions immediately tangible. To ensure relevance, the communication addressed real-life concerns such as physical strain and early retirement.

We recommended creating a "safe space" for learning, characterized by accessible language and a low barrier to entry, ensuring that all users - regardless of professional background -felt empowered to take charge of their financial future.

Gamified Visualization

Transformed abstract data into an interactive, visual overview, making the long-term impact of today’s choices immediately tangible.

Relatable Context

Anchored the dialogue in real-world concerns, such as physical wear and tear and the desire for early retirement.

Radical Accessibility

Prioritized low-complexity language and a "safe space" for questions to dismantle the formal barriers of the pension industry.

The app

The core function of Horisont was to empower users to articulate their personal expectations and dreams for retirement. By prioritizing human needs over abstract numbers, the solution made pension planning personal, relatable, and present.

Once users defined their aspirations, the platform enabled them to assess the feasibility of their dreams and adjust their financial habits accordingly. By bridging the gap between thought and action, Horisont transformed vague retirement ideas into concrete goals, fostering clarity, motivation, and proactive decision-making.

Personal Visioning

Users articulated their own dreams and expectations for life after work.

Relatable Planning

Shifted the focus from abstract data to personal relevance, making pensions tangible rather than intimidating.

From Thought to Action

Converted retirement thoughts into concrete milestones, driving both motivation and immediate action.

The Mental Health Waiting Game

The mental health sector is currently facing a global crisis, characterized by excessive waiting lists that leave individuals struggling with anxiety without immediate support. This gap in the healthcare system has driven a rising trend where users turn to Artificial Intelligence as a surrogate for traditional therapy.

While AI offers accessibility, it introduces critical challenges regarding safety and ethical responsibility. This project explores the tension between the urgent need for anxiety management and the necessity of creating a responsible, AI-driven support tool that provides value without compromising mental well-being.

What we found

The "Support Vacuum": There is a critical gap between the initial realization of anxiety and the start of professional treatment. Users often feel left in a "support vacuum" where symptoms can escalate due to the lack of immediate, low-threshold tools.

The Need for Cognitive Distraction: Anxiety is often fueled by intrusive thought loops. We found a high demand for "active grounding"—tools that don't just track mood, but actively help users "get out of their heads" by facilitating immediate mental shifts.

Personalization as Trust: Generic mental health apps often feel clinical and detached. Our insights showed that trust is built through personalization. By using an LLM to facilitate dialogue, the solution moves from being a static tool to a dynamic companion that understands the user’s specific context.

AI Mental Health Companion

Our concept is a mobile application that bridges the gap in mental health support through a combination of proactive nudging and reactive AI dialogue. At its heart is an LLM-powered chatbot that facilitates supportive conversations, helping users navigate anxious moments through real-time interaction.

To complement the dialogue, the app features a suite of grounding tools, such as breathing exercises and metacognitive techniques. These are supported by an intelligent calendar function that nudges the user toward consistent practice, ensuring that mental well-being becomes a manageable, daily routine.

Co-regulate through Dialogue

The chatbot engages users in supportive conversations, helping them articulate their feelings and de-escalate anxiety in the moment.

Personalized Guidance

By leveraging Natural Language Processing (NLP), the bot can suggest specific breathing or metacognitive exercises based on the context of the conversation.

Lowering the Barrier to Support

For users who find it difficult to speak to a person, the chatbot provides a "safe space" to practice self-reflection and coping strategies.

Whirl

Whirl is a proactive mental health application designed to act as a bridge for individuals navigating anxiety. By combining intelligent scheduling with real-time AI support and immersive exercises, Whirl transforms passive waiting time into an active journey toward better mental well-being.

Conceptually, Whirl is built on the principle of Co-regulation. By synchronizing technology with human physiology (breathing) and daily life (calendar), the app moves from being a simple utility to a dynamic companion.

Using Botpress allowed us to bridge the gap between advanced LLM capabilities and clinical safety, ensuring that the AI’s responses remain focused on de-escalation and supportive guidance without the risks often associated with open-ended AI models.

Intelligent Calendar Synchronization

Users can schedule specific "mental pitstops" and receive proactive reminders, ensuring that self-care is woven into their daily routine rather than being an afterthought.

Context-Aware Mental Exercises

Based on the user’s schedule, Whirl suggests specific exercises tailored to the situation.

Controlled AI Dialogue (Botpress Implementation)

The heart of the interaction is a custom-built chatbot developed with Botpress. By using a controlled environment, we ensure an extra layer of "safety" and reliability.
